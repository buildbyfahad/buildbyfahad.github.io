How I work

The process

No mystery, no disappearing for three weeks.

  1. 01

    Understand

    We talk through the problem, the users, and the constraints before a line of code exists. Scope gets written down.

  2. 02

    Plan

    Data model, architecture, and milestones. You see the shape of the thing and what each phase delivers.

  3. 03

    Build

    Work ships in visible increments with a live preview URL. You review as it goes — no big-bang reveal at the end.

  4. 04

    Ship & support

    Deploy, monitoring, and handover docs. I stay reachable after launch, because that's when real bugs show up.
